## 🧩 Problem Statement

> **“Introduction to GenAI and Simple LLM Inference on CPU and Finetuning of LLM — Model to create a Custom Chatbot”**

### Challenges:
- 📚 **Lack of Accessible Legal Information:** Difficulty finding reliable, easy-to-understand legal content.  
- ⚖️ **Complex Legal Terminology:** Legal texts are often dense and expert-oriented.  
- 🧠 **Difficulty in Legal Research:** Legal analysis takes time and expertise.  
- 🪶 **Limited Legal Resources:** Few all-in-one solutions for ordinary users.  
- 🔍 **Need for Document Analysis:** Lawyers and students need fast comparison tools.  

---

## 💡 Unique Idea Brief (Solution)

**Sahi Jawab** is an intelligent legal assistant designed to make Indian law **simpler, accessible, and interactive** for everyone.  

### Key Highlights:
- 🧾 **Comprehensive Legal Platform:** Unified, user-friendly legal database.  
- ⚖️ **Embedded Legal Texts:** Integrated three new Indian criminal laws —  
  *Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)*, *Bharatiya Nagrik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS)*, and *Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA)*.  
- 🔍 **Document Similarity Search:** Compare and analyze similarities or differences between legal documents.  
- 🤖 **Interactive Query System:** Ask your question — get AI-generated, concise answers.  
- 🚀 **Future Enhancements:** Translation, lawyer connections, instant talks, and a rich repository of judgments.

## 🔄 Process Flow

1. **User Interaction** 🤝 → User submits a legal query or uploads documents.  
2. **Vector Embeddings** 🔍 → Legal texts are embedded into vector space for semantic understanding.  
3. **Query Processing** ⚙️ → The system interprets and processes the query using LLM.  
4. **Information Retrieval** 📚 → Retrieves the most relevant legal insights or document comparisons.  
5. **Response Generation** ✉ → Outputs a concise, structured legal answer.  
6. **User Engagement** 💬 → Enables follow-up questions and deeper legal exploration.

## 🏗 Architecture Overview

**Frontend:**  
> Built using **Streamlit**, ensuring a clean and responsive interface.  

**Backend:**  
> Powered by **Llama3** through **GROQ API** for ultra-fast inference.  

**Data Storage:**  
> Vector databases store legal texts and embeddings locally for privacy and performance.  

**APIs Used:**  
- ⚡ **GROQ API** – For Llama3 model inference  
- 🧠 **Google Embedding Model** – For semantic document search  

**Integration:**  
> Tight linkage between frontend, backend, and APIs builds an **end-to-end AI Legal Chatbot**.
